A Walk in Central Park

A small field journal from New York City

September 21, 2026

I walked around Central Park and tried to pay attention to things I normally walk past without noticing. The park felt very different from the streets of Manhattan.

What I Saw

  • Big trees and small plants
  • People walking their dogs
  • Runners and people exercising
  • Tourists taking pictures
  • People sitting and talking on the grass

My Observations

🌳 Trees

There were many different shapes and sizes of trees. Some areas felt very quiet because the trees blocked the view of the city.

🐿️ Animals

I noticed squirrels moving quickly between the trees. They seemed very comfortable around people.

👟 People

I saw many different kinds of people. Some were walking quickly, while others were sitting on benches or relaxing on the grass.

One Moment I Remember

"For a moment, I forgot that I was in the middle of New York City."

The sounds of the city became quieter when I walked deeper into the park. I could hear birds, people talking, and the wind in the trees. It made the park feel like a small different world inside the city.
